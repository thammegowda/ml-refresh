= Probability Theory

== Keep in mind

=== Area is probability
A probability density is not itself a probability. For a continuous random variable, probability is the area under the density over an interval; the probability at one exact point is zero.

=== Location and spread
The normal distribution's mean mu sets its center. Its standard deviation sigma must be positive and controls its spread. The total area under the density is one.

=== Standardize to compare
The z-score z = (x - mu) / sigma measures how many standard deviations a value lies from the mean. About 68% of a normal distribution lies within one standard deviation of its mean.
