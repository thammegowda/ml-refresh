Temperature divides the logits before softmax. With stem:[T=1], stem:[(4,1,-1)] is sharply peaked
on the first class. With larger stem:[T], the same ordering remains, but probability moves from the
top class into the lower classes.

[#sol-ex-distillation-t2.solution]
====
For stem:[\vp_T=\softmax(\vz^s/T)], the usual softmax-cross-entropy derivative with respect to
stem:[\vz^s/T] is stem:[\vp_T-\vq_T]. By the chain rule,

[latexmath]
++++
\frac{\partial}{\partial \vz^s}
\Big[-\sum_k q_{T,k}\log p_{T,k}\Big]
= \frac{\vp_T-\vq_T}{T}.
++++

Multiplying the objective by stem:[T^2] gives stem:[T(\vp_T-\vq_T)]. Since the difference between
softened distributions shrinks roughly like stem:[1/T], the multiplier keeps gradients from
vanishing as temperature rises.

[#sol-ex-distillation-voting.solution]
====
For majority vote with stem:[p=0.6] and stem:[n=5], sum the cases with three, four, or five
correct samples:

[latexmath]
++++
{5\choose3}0.6^3 0.4^2 + {5\choose4}0.6^4 0.4 + 0.6^5 = 0.68256.
++++

For best-of-stem:[5] with a perfect selector,

[latexmath]
++++
1 - (1 - 0.6)^5 = 0.98976.
++++
