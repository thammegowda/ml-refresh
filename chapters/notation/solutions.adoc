[#sol-ex-notation-affine.solution]
====
stem:[X_{ij}] appears in every output of row stem:[i]: stem:[Y_{ik} = \sum_{j'} X_{ij'} W_{j'k} + b_k],
so stem:[\partial Y_{ik} / \partial X_{ij} = W_{jk}], and outputs of other rows do not depend on
it. By the chain rule,

[latexmath]
++++
\bar{X}_{ij} = \sum_{k} \bar{Y}_{ik} W_{jk} = \sum_k \bar{Y}_{ik} (\mW^\T)_{kj} = (\bar{\mY} \mW^\T)_{ij} .
++++

To check it, fix a random stem:[\mG] and treat stem:[L(\mX) = \sum G \odot \operatorname{affine}(\mX)]
as the loss. Its exact gradient is `affine_backward(G, X, W)[0]`, which `check_gradient` compares
with central differences. The book's tests run this check for stem:[\mX], stem:[\mW], and
stem:[\vb].
====
